## BPE vs WordPiece
BPE grows its vocabulary by repeatedly merging the most frequent adjacent symbol pair (pure frequency). WordPiece instead adds the merge that most increases the training corpus likelihood under a unigram language model (a likelihood/association score, roughly `count(ab) / (count(a) * count(b))`), not raw frequency alone. BERT's WordPiece marks every non-word-initial subword with a leading `##`.
